Proctoring Notice

This assessment is video-proctored. Your camera and microphone will be active for the entire duration. The session will be recorded for verification and moderation purposes.

  • 1 You have 60 minutes to complete 50 multiple-choice questions. The timer starts once you click Start Assessment.
  • 2 Your face must remain visible to the camera at all times. If your face is not detected for an extended period, your assessment will be terminated.

  • 4 Ensure you are in a quiet, well-lit environment. A camera that is too dark will trigger a violation warning.
  • 5 Do not close or refresh your browser until after submission. Doing so may result in a lost attempt.
  • 6 Accumulated proctoring violations will lead to automatic removal from the assessment and your attempt may be forfeited with a score of 0%.
